# Quobot — Today Trader market analysis bot

A market analysis bot for the assets you trade on Quotex (forex pairs, gold, indices, crypto).
It pulls public price data, runs common technical indicators and prints a CALL / PUT / WAIT
bias for each asset, with the reasons behind it. It can watch the market and send Telegram
alerts, and it can backtest its own signals so you can see whether they would have made money.

> **Not financial advice.** The bot reads indicators on public market data; it does not predict
> the future. Binary options are high risk and most traders lose money. Never trade money you
> can't afford to lose.

## What it does not do

- **It does not place trades on Quotex.** Quotex has no public API, and automating its site would
  break its terms. You make every trade yourself.
- **It can't analyse Quotex OTC assets** (the ones marked "OTC", mostly at weekends). Those prices
  are generated by the broker and aren't on any public feed. Use the regular market pairs.
- Prices come from Yahoo Finance (forex, metals, indices) and Binance (crypto). Quotex's quotes can
  differ slightly from these.

## Use

```bash
# Scan the default watchlist on 5-minute candles
python -m today_trader scan

# Your own assets and timeframe (1m 2m 5m 15m 30m 1h)
python -m today_trader scan EURUSD GBPJPY XAUUSD BTCUSDT -i 1m

# Show the reasons for every asset, not just the ones with a bias
python -m today_trader scan -v

# Re-scan every time a candle closes (alerts when 2+ of 4 groups agree)
python -m today_trader watch EURUSD GBPUSD -i 1m

# Only show signals where 3 of 4 groups agree
python -m today_trader scan --min-agree 3

# Check how the signals would have done on recent history
python -m today_trader backtest EURUSD GBPUSD -i 1m --expiry 1 --payout 0.85

# Plain-language market read: prediction, reasons, what would prove it wrong, track record
python -m today_trader read EURUSD XAUUSD -i 1m --expiry 1

# Learn from up to 10,000 past candles: best settings, checked on data it never saw
python -m today_trader study EURUSD GBPUSD XAUUSD -i 1m

# Stake size and daily stop for your balance
python -m today_trader risk 100

# Try it offline with synthetic data
python -m today_trader scan --demo
```

### Automatic safe picks (start-bot.bat)

```bash
python -m today_trader bot -i 1m --picks-from all --picks-every 5 --top 3
```

Every 5 minutes the bot scans every asset and sends you only the trades that pass **all** safety
checks, ranked by a safety score (0-100). Each pick says:

- the **asset** and **CALL ⬆️ / PUT ⬇️**
- the **candle** timeframe and the **expiry** to set
- the **entry time** and **end time** in Pakistan time
- the current price, the "wrong if" level, agreement and track record
- **🧠 Why UP / Why DOWN:** the reasons in plain sentences (most important first: candle pattern,
  support/resistance, divergence, trend, momentum), plus the main **⚠️ Risk** against it

A pick must have 3+ of 4 groups agreeing, a normal market (not against the bigger trend, not
too quiet or spiking, good session, market open), a track record above break-even, past studies
that held up on unseen data, and similar past moments leaning the same way. Most rounds have
0-1 picks; "No safe trades right now" is a normal answer.

After each pick's expiry, the bot checks the closing price and sends **✅ WIN / ❌ LOSS** with
today's score. Every pick is saved in `quobot_picks.csv` as your journal. `/picks` runs a round
now and `/results` shows today's wins and losses. Results use public prices, which can differ a
little from Quotex's.

Each pick shows a **🎯 win chance** with a likely range, taken from real history (the study's
result on unseen data, or the asset's track record). **Strict mode** `--min-winrate 0.6` only
sends picks whose estimated win chance is 60% or more; expect very few picks. No method gives a
real 90%: anything that claims it is overfitted or a scam.

Options: `--picks-every 10` (minutes, 0 = off), `--top 5`, `--quiet` (no message when there are
no picks), `--alerts` (also alert on every watchlist signal, the old behaviour).

## How the score works

The bot follows a day trader's checklist. Each candle gets a score from -100 (strongly down) to
+100 (strongly up).

**1. Four groups of votes**

| Group | What it checks | Counts more when |
|---|---|---|
| Trend | EMA 9/21, price vs EMA 50, +DI/-DI, Supertrend, Heikin Ashi | ADX > 25 (trending) |
| Momentum | MACD histogram and its slope | always |
| Reversal | RSI 30/70, Bollinger bands, Stochastic 20/80, CCI +-100, Williams %R | ADX < 25 (ranging) |
| Price action | Candlestick patterns, support/resistance bounce or breakout, RSI divergence | always |

Candlestick patterns: engulfing, pin bar (hammer / shooting star), doji, morning / evening star,
three white soldiers / black crows, piercing line / dark cloud cover, inside-bar breakout.

Support and resistance come from clusters of recent swing highs and lows. The scan prints the two
nearest levels on each side and how many times price touched them.

**2. Filters** lower the score when conditions are poor:

- **Higher timeframe:** the score is halved if the trend on a 5x higher timeframe (1m -> 5m) disagrees.
- **Volatility:** reduced when the market is very quiet or spiking (often news).
- **Session:** full weight in the London / New York overlap, reduced in Asian and late hours,
  zero for forex at weekends (Quotex only offers OTC then).

**3. Agreement:** "Agree 3/4" means three of the four groups back the bias. `watch` only alerts
when at least 2 of 4 agree (change with `--min-agree`).

A score of +40 or more shows **CALL (up)**, -40 or less shows **PUT (down)**, anything in between
shows **WAIT**. Change the cut-off with `-t`, e.g. `-t 60` for fewer, stronger signals.

The bot never uses information it couldn't have had at the time. Swing points only count once
they're confirmed, the unfinished current candle is ignored, and the tests check this
(look-ahead bias is the usual reason trading bots look great in backtests and then lose live).

## Market read

`read` explains the market in plain words for each asset:

- **What the market is doing:** trend, trend strength, the higher-timeframe trend, momentum,
  nearest support and resistance, the last candle pattern, and volatility.
- **Prediction:** UP, DOWN or NO CLEAR EDGE for the next `--expiry` candles.
- **Why / Against it:** every indicator that supports the call, and every one that disagrees.
- **Wrong if:** the price level that cancels the idea (nearest support for UP, resistance for DOWN).
- **Typical move:** how far price usually moves in that time (from ATR).
- **Track record:** how often strong signals in the same direction were right on this asset's
  recent history, compared with the win rate you need to break even, for 1, 2, 3 and 5 candles.

Telegram alerts from `watch` send this same read.

The "prediction" is a lean based on indicators, not a forecast anyone can guarantee. Trust the
track-record line more than the prediction: if it says BELOW break-even, skip that asset.

## Learning from history (`study`)

`study` replays up to 10,000 past candles of an asset as if each one were live and records what
would have happened to every signal. It tries 48 setting combinations (signal strength 30-60,
2 or 3 of 4 groups agreeing or not, expiry 1/2/3/5 candles) and keeps the best one.

To avoid fooling itself, it picks the settings on the **older 70%** of the data and then checks
them on the **newer 30% it never saw**. Only that second number tells you how the settings are
likely to do from here. If they fail on unseen data, the bot says so and stops alerting on that
asset. Settings are saved in `quobot_settings.json` and used automatically. The `bot` command runs
a study for every asset on your watchlist when it starts.

Every market read also has an **Experience** section: the bot finds the ~150 past moments that
looked most like right now (same indicator votes, RSI, trend strength, volatility, bigger trend,
time of day) and shows how often price went up or down afterwards, with a likely range.

How much history you get: Yahoo keeps 1-minute forex data for about 30 days (roughly 10,000
candles on weekdays), 5-minute for 60 days. Crypto from Binance goes back as far as you need.

## Time zone

All times are shown in Pakistan time (UTC+5) by default. Each signal says when to enter (the start
of the next candle), what expiry to set, when the trade ends, and when it's too late to enter.
To use another time zone, add `TIMEZONE=Asia/Dubai` (or similar) to `.env`. Send `/sessions` to
the bot for session times; the London + New York overlap (about 5-9 PM in Pakistan) is usually
the best time to trade forex.

## Risk management

```bash
python -m today_trader risk 100              # balance of 100
python -m today_trader risk 500 --risk 2     # 2% per trade
```

It prints your stake per trade, when to stop for the day, the win rate you need to break even,
and what a losing streak costs with and without martingale. Day-trader rules built in:
risk 1-2% per trade, stop after 3 losses in a row or 5% down, never double up after a loss.

## Where the ideas come from

The techniques were chosen by looking at the most-starred open-source trading projects on GitHub,
such as [freqtrade-strategies](https://github.com/freqtrade/freqtrade-strategies) (most-used
indicators: EMA, RSI, Bollinger, ADX, MACD, CCI, Supertrend, Heikin Ashi, multi-timeframe and
hour filters, and its notes on look-ahead bias) and
[pandas-ta-classic](https://github.com/xgboosted/pandas-ta-classic) (candlestick pattern set).
All code here was written from scratch; nothing was copied from those projects.

## Read the backtest before you trust a signal

With an 85% payout you need to win more than about 54% of trades just to break even. The backtest
prints that break-even line, and shows results with and without the filters so you can see
whether they help on your asset. Short timeframes (1m) are close to random,
so expect win rates near 50%. If the signals don't beat break-even on an asset and timeframe, don't
trade them there.
